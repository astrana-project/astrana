# 98. Resharing something publishes it anew, to the resharer's own audience

Proposed.

## Context

A connection reaches only the two people in it ([record 36](0036-a-connection-reaches-only-the-two-people-in-it.md)),
and every post has an audience chosen by its author ([record 48](0048-the-owner-chooses-who-each-post-is-for.md)).
Passing something on has to fit both.

## Decision

A reshare is a new act of publication. It goes to the resharer's own peers, under their own audience settings, never to
an audience the original author did not choose.

## Consequences

- Every step of spreading needs a person deciding to make something their own, to an audience they chose.
