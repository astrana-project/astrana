# 84. A child can always see their guardian's oversight

Proposed.

## Context

The tools that protect a child from strangers can be turned on the child by a guardian
([record 34](0034-when-a-child-owns-an-instance-a-parent-or-guardian-holds-authority-over-its-parental-controls.md)).

## Decision

Guardian oversight is visible to the child and never hidden. The child sees what the guardian sees, when the guardian
looks, and the guardian's sign-ins.

## Consequences

- Oversight a child can see is a different thing from oversight they cannot, and hidden monitoring is the pattern this
  guards against most.
- This does not protect a child from a guardian who is the source of harm. It makes the arrangement visible, which is
  the protection available.
