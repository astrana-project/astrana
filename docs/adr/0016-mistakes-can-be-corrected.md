# 16. Mistakes can be corrected

Proposed.

## Context

Every interface assumes a person who understood the consequences and meant what they did. Often they did not.

## Decision

Astrana is designed so that a mistake can be corrected, for the person who did the wrong thing as well as the one who
did the right thing.

## Consequences

- Astrana can correct its own record, but it cannot un-share, un-see or reach beyond itself, and it says so plainly.
- This covers social mistakes as well as practical ones, such as something said in anger.
