# 30. A child can own an instance, and age is declared rather than verified

Proposed.

## Context

Children use social media, and an instance belongs to one person
([record 1](0001-each-instance-has-one-owner-who-is-its-sole-administrator.md)).

## Decision

A child can be, and typically is, the owner of their own instance. Their age is declared, and nothing verifies it.

## Consequences

- No approach available to a self-hosted project can reliably check age, and Astrana does not claim to.
- Protections for children rest on an honest declaration, so they will not stop a determined teenager.
