# 49. No engagement count is presented as an achievement

Proposed.

## Context

A feed that ranks nothing ([record 18](0018-the-feed-is-chronological-and-never-ranked.md)) can still reward reach if it
presents engagement as success.

## Decision

Astrana never presents engagement, such as how many people reacted or how far something spread, as an achievement.

## Consequences

- Nothing rewards reach, so nothing pushes people to chase it.
- Counts of reactions and likes are still visible to everyone, as a plain record of how people responded.
