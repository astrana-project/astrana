# 100. There is no reputation system

Proposed.

## Context

Each instance keeps its own record of what was done to its owner
([record 9](0009-every-material-action-is-logged-and-the-log-never-copies-content.md)), so evidence of someone behaving
badly towards many people exists across many instances
([record 59](0059-signals-about-a-peer-inform-the-owner-and-never-act-by-themselves.md)).

## Decision

Nothing gathers what different instances have recorded about someone into a score or a shared record.

## Consequences

- A pattern becomes known the way patterns always have, through people who know each other talking.
- A score would be gameable and permanent, and would stand in for the judgement of people who actually know the person.
- Each person meets someone acting badly alone, unless people talk to each other, and a pattern spread across many
  instances is harder to see.
