# 101. A report goes to the owner of the instance, never to the project

Proposed.

## Context

Someone who sees a problem with content needs somewhere to raise it. The owner of the instance it is on already decides
what stays there
([record 43](0043-the-owner-of-an-instance-may-remove-anything-stored-on-it-except-a-report-about-their-content.md)).

## Decision

A report about content goes to the owner of the instance it is on. It never goes to Astrana or to any central authority.
Nothing acts automatically on reports, and the owner is not obliged to act.

## Consequences

- There is no central review queue, and no action triggered by the number of reports.
- Reports inform the person with authority over the space, who decides what to do.
- When the owner wrote the reported content, the report reaches nobody who would act on it.
- Illegal content has no route beyond the owner of the instance and the authorities.
