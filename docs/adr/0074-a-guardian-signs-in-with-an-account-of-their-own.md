# 74. A guardian signs in with an account of their own

Proposed.

## Context

A guardian's authority over a child's parental controls
([record 30](0030-when-a-child-owns-an-instance-a-parent-or-guardian-holds-authority-over-its-parental-controls.md)) has
to be exercised through some account.

## Decision

A parent or guardian has a separate account with its own sign-in on the child's instance. They never act through the
child's account or through a token borrowed from it.

## Consequences

- Everything a guardian does is done as the guardian, and recorded as theirs.
