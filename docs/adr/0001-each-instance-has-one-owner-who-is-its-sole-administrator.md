# 1. Each instance has one owner, who is its sole administrator

Proposed.

## Context

Astrana's design rests on the view that the individual is the basic unit of society. Families, communities and every
other group are made up of individuals. The groups themselves, and their social norms, customs and rules, arise from the
voluntary connections between those individuals rather than from a structure above them. An instance is one installation
of Astrana, and it represents and belongs to one person.

## Decision

Each instance has one owner, who is its only administrator. There is no operator and no authority of any kind above the
owner. Guest users can use an instance too, but authority over it never leaves the owner. The one exception is a child's
instance, where a parent or guardian has authority over its parental controls and nothing else.

## Consequences

- Nobody can suspend an owner or their instance, because nobody holds authority above them.
- An owner runs a service of their own and answers for it. A takedown notice, a rights claim or a police enquiry reaches
  them directly, because there is nobody in between.
- There is no support service to turn to if something breaks.
