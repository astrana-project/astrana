# 77. Several guardians may hold equal authority

Proposed.

## Context

A child often has more than one parent or guardian
([record 31](0031-when-a-child-owns-an-instance-a-parent-or-guardian-holds-authority-over-its-parental-controls.md)).

## Decision

More than one parent or guardian may have an account on the same child's instance, each with equal and independent
authority.

## Consequences

- Two parents can each have their own account, and neither needs the other's agreement to act.
- Guardians can undo each other's choices, and the software does not settle disagreements between them.
