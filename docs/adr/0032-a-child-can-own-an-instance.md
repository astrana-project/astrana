# 32. A child can own an instance

Proposed.

## Context

Children use social media, and an instance belongs to one person
([record 1](0001-each-instance-has-one-owner-who-is-its-sole-administrator.md)). A child could be given an account on
someone else's instance, or own an instance of their own.

## Decision

A child can be the owner of their own instance.

## Consequences

- A child's content, connections and history are theirs, and stay with them when they come of age.
- Protections for children have to work on an instance the child owns, so they rest on authority given to someone else
  over part of it.
- Giving children only accounts on an adult's instance was rejected, because it would make a child's social life part of
  someone else's.
