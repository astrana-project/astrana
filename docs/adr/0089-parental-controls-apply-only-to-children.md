# 89. Parental controls apply only to children

Proposed.

## Context

The tools a guardian uses to protect a child
([record 34](0034-when-a-child-owns-an-instance-a-parent-or-guardian-holds-authority-over-its-parental-controls.md))
could be turned into control over anyone.

## Decision

Overriding privacy, restricting content and limiting features exist only to protect children. They are never a
capability anyone holds over an adult.

## Consequences

- [Record 31](0034-when-a-child-owns-an-instance-a-parent-or-guardian-holds-authority-over-its-parental-controls.md)
  limits what a guardian may do, and this record limits the controls themselves. No setting, configuration or kind of
  account turns them on for an adult's instance, a custodian's account included.
- Adults keep full control of their own instance, including the freedom to take risks Astrana does not try to prevent.
