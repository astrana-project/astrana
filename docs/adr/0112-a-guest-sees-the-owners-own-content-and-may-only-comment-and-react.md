# 112. A guest sees the owner's own content and may only comment and react

Proposed.

## Context

Some people close to an owner will never run an instance of their own.

## Decision

An owner may give such a person a guest account. A guest sees the owner's own content that is shared with them, and may
comment and react, but can post nothing. They never see what peers shared, the owner's peers or the feed
([record 75](0075-an-account-gets-only-what-its-role-needs-settled-in-advance.md)).

## Consequences

- Someone who will not run an instance can still stay in touch.
- A guest relates to one person, not to that person's network.
