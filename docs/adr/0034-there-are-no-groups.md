# 34. There are no groups

Proposed.

## Context

Some things look like groups, such as a school's or a community's private network. A group could be built as a shared
thing of its own, or as many people each connected to one another
([record 2](0002-connections-are-mutual-and-nobody-follows-anyone.md)).

## Decision

Astrana has no groups. Every connection is between two people, and what looks like a group is many separate one-to-one
connections.

## Consequences

- A school or community network is made of individual connections, each approved by both of the people in it.
- A network of many people needs every pair of them to connect, and there is no shared space that belongs to them all.
- A group as a shared thing of its own was rejected, because it would have to live on someone's instance or a central
  service, and would give whoever held it authority over what the others share there.
