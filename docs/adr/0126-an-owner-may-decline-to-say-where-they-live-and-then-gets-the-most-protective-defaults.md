# 126. An owner may decline to say where they live, and then gets the most protective defaults

Proposed.

## Context

Some owners will have good reason not to say where they live, and a declaration cannot be checked
([record 113](0113-a-jurisdiction-selects-a-small-set-of-values-not-special-behaviour.md)).

## Decision

An owner may decline to say where they live. A universal profile then applies, set to the protective side, with Coming
of Age at 18 and the age of digital consent at 16.

## Consequences

- Lying never pays. Claiming a more permissive jurisdiction gets the same as declining, and claiming a stricter one gets
  stricter treatment.
