# Mission and vision

Every [decision record](adr/README.md) rests on the mission and vision below. A record that cannot be traced back to
them needs a reason of its own.

## Mission

To build social networking software that is materially healthier than what people have now, by shaping the technology to
the whole of human nature rather than to whichever parts of it are commercially profitable, and to the interests of the
people using it rather than anybody else's.

## Vision

A place of your own, for the people already well known to you, where relationships are tended and deepened over a
lifetime. Yours to own, never competing for your attention, seen only by the people each thing is intended for, and
keeping only what matters.

## The premise

The individual is the basic unit of society. Each person runs their own instance of Astrana, which is free and open
source. Instances connect directly to each other, with no third party required in between. A child can have an instance
too, with a parent or guardian holding authority over its protections.

## The commitments

### For the people already well known to you

There is no discovery, no directory and no strangers. You connect with someone only when you both agree.

The cost is that people reach you only through an address you give them, and Astrana never suggests anyone to connect
with.

### Yours to own

Your instance runs on hardware or hosting you choose, and no platform sits above you, so nobody can suspend you.

The cost is that you are responsible for your instance, and look after it yourself or have someone you trust do it for
you.

### Never competing for your attention

Nothing is ranked to hold your attention, and nothing invents a reason to come back, such as a streak or a reminder. The
feed stops when you reach the end, rather than scrolling forever.

The cost is that you can miss what someone shares unless you go back and look for it, because Astrana will not remind
you about it.

### Seen only by the people each thing is intended for

When you share something, you choose who can see it, such as your family or a few close friends, and nobody else can.

The cost is a small choice each time you share, which Astrana keeps light by remembering the last one you made.

### Keeping only what matters

What you share disappears after a set time unless you deliberately choose to keep it. Things meant to last, such as
albums, stay until you remove them.

The cost is that anything not kept is deleted when its time is up.

## When things conflict

When two good things pull in different directions, the choice that is better for the wellbeing of the people using
Astrana wins. When a technically better design works against the mission, the mission wins, even if the result is
slower, plainer or harder to build.

Some things are never traded away:

- Success is never measured by how much people use Astrana.
- Nothing makes money from the time people spend in it.
- Nobody's choices are taken away to protect them from every risk.
- Astrana never claims to protect people more than it does.
