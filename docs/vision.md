# Vision

Every [decision record](adr/README.md) rests on the mission and vision below. A record that cannot be traced back to
them needs a reason of its own.

## Mission

To build social networking software that is materially healthier than what people have now, by shaping the technology to
the whole of human nature rather than to the parts of it that can be exploited.

## Vision

A place of your own, for the people already well known to you, where relationships are tended and deepened over years
rather than kept up with. Yours to own, seen only by the people each thing is for, and keeping only what matters.

## The premise

The individual is the basic unit of society. Each person runs their own instance of Astrana and connects it directly to
the instances of people they already know well, with no organisation in between.

## The four commitments

### For the people already well known to you

There is no discovery, no directory and no strangers. Two instances connect only when both owners agree.

The cost is that nobody can find you, and a new instance starts empty.

### Yours to own

An instance runs on hardware or hosting its owner chooses, and the owner is its only administrator, so nobody can
suspend them.

The cost is that nobody is coming to help either.

### Seen only by the people each thing is for

Every post goes to one or more audiences, and nobody outside them sees it.

The cost is that a post goes to every connected peer unless the owner narrows it.

### Keeping only what matters

Forgetting is the default, and keeping is a choice.

The cost is that content you did not think to keep is gone.
